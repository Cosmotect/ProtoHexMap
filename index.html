How to play

Goal: you start on the bright tile in the centre of the map. The boss is hidden somewhere on the outer edge. Fog hides the map until you get next to it.

Move: click a glowing neighbouring tile. Stepping on an encounter does not start it: press Engage (or E) to enter it, or walk on.

Encounters: Battle runs an automatic fight (your units vs enemies, damage depends on power). Treasure gives supplies. Shop sells a rest (fatigue reset) and unit upgrades (+1 power). Events tell a story and then do something: reveal parts of the map, hand out supplies or power, offer a dark bargain, or turn into a fight. Mountains can be crossed for 10 supplies and 5 HP per unit, and you see 2 rings further from the top. The Acolyte of the Great Forge restores a fallen unit. On an empty tile, Engage makes camp for supplies and heals every living unit by half its max HP.

Fatigue: every step since your last encounter tires the party. After a few free steps, arriving on an encounter tile has a growing chance of forcing you into it. Hover a tile to see the chance. Engaging any encounter resets fatigue.

Camera: left-drag or arrow keys to pan, right-drag to rotate and tilt, mouse wheel to zoom. Press C to switch between the perspective and top-down cameras.

Share a map: the seed in the address bar (?seed=1234) recreates the exact same map. Use "Copy link".

Encounters are placeholders: engaging one only writes a log line and clears the marker for now.
